## 2024-05-18 - Privacy for Sensitive Inputs
**Learning:** For privacy and security, input fields and textareas containing sensitive user data (e.g., medical or allergy notes) should have spellcheck disabled to prevent sensitive data from being sent to third-party spellcheck services.
**Action:** Use `spellcheck="false"` on sensitive inputs (like those in `skin_profile/code.html`) going forward.
