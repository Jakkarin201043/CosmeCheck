## 2024-05-16 - Add ARIA Labels to Icon-Only Social Sign-in & Visibility Toggles
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons or buttons with just a single character (like 'G' for Google or the iOS icon for Apple) used for social logins or password visibility toggles lack context for screen readers. If the internal element (like a `span` with an icon or single letter) isn't hidden from screen readers, it can result in confusing announcements (e.g., just announcing "G" or "visibility_off" without the context of it being a button to "Continue with Google" or "Toggle password visibility").
**Action:** Always add descriptive `aria-label` attributes to the `<button>` element and set `aria-hidden="true"` on the internal `<span>` containing the icon/text to ensure proper and clear screen reader announcements for icon-only/single-character buttons.
